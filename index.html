How I Work

Find the real problem first

Complexity lives in the connections

Design for the person, not the flow

01 /

Not every brief is wrong, but enough are. Before anything takes shape, I make sure the team is solving the real problem: clearly defined, genuinely understood, and agreed on across PM, Engineering, and Design. A well-built wrong solution is harder to kill than a bad one.

Every project gets the approach it needs.

I don't run one fixed process. The shape changes with the problem. Pick an approach, then select a phase to see how it breaks down.

Typical timeline

For a focused scope, adapted to product maturity, risk, access to users, and team capacity. Overlaps compress calendar time; they don't shrink the work.

How I integrate AI

AI supports each phase above: synthesis, exploring flows, drafting specs, surfacing signals. I still own the judgment calls, especially where a phase's "human checkpoint" applies.

About

Desi Lazova

Lead Product Designer · Sofia

LinkedIn ↗

Simplifying complexity for people - in the age of AI.

Started as a graphic designer - studios, agencies, freelance, corporate identities, visual systems across different industries and constraints. That foundation still shows up in everything I do: a strong visual language and a real eye for aesthetics aren't extras in product design. They're how you earn trust from users who have to live inside your decisions every day.

What pulled me toward product design was the question underneath the interface - how does this system actually work, why does it break, and what do the people using it really need. Over the last ten years I've built products from zero, evaluated complex existing systems, and created design systems from scratch. Different contexts, different industries - the same core interest: taking complexity and translating it into workflows where users can act with confidence.

What I'm looking for now: remote-first, professional users, systems complex enough that design decisions actually matter. Less about the title, more about being trusted with the hard problems.
